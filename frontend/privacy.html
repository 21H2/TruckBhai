Privacy policy

Sample policy for the TruckBhai demo. Not legal advice.

What we collect

Three forms on this site collect information. A booking records the vehicle, pickup and drop-off locations and your rental dates. A contact message records your name, email and the message text. A newsletter subscription records your email address only.

Why we collect it

Bookings let us arrange a vehicle and contact you about it. Messages let us answer your enquiry. Subscriptions let us send offers you asked for.

Where it is stored

Submissions are written to the application database, or to local JSON files when no database is configured. Every record carries a generated reference and a UTC timestamp.

Who can see it

Only staff signed in to the admin dashboard can read submissions, and that dashboard is protected by a password and an expiring session. Submission files are never served over the web.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as needed to run the rental and meet accounting obligations, and remove newsletter addresses when you unsubscribe.

Your choices

Ask us to correct or delete your details at any time. Unsubscribing stops newsletter email without affecting a booking already in progress.

Privacy questions? Contact us or email info@truckbhai.com.
