Help centre

Answers to what people ask us most. Still stuck? Send us a message and a representative will get back to you.

How do I book a truck?

Use the booking form on the home page. Pick a vehicle, enter your pickup and drop-off locations and choose your dates, then press Preview trip to see the road route, drive time and fare estimate before you confirm.

How is the fare estimated?

We take the road distance for your route, the current diesel price for your pickup city and the consumption of the vehicle you chose, then add the daily rental for the number of days between your pickup and return dates. It is an estimate, not a final invoice.

Where do I find my booking reference?

It appears on screen as soon as a booking is saved, and it looks like 84fbd27f-1d43-4d41-adcc-e64e7580ba09. Keep it: the tracking page needs the full reference to look your booking up.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Contact us with your reference. Cancelling at least 24 hours before pickup returns any deposit in full, as set out in the terms.

Which vehicle should I pick?

A TATA Ace suits small household moves, an EICHER Pro or EICHER 19 suits full house shifts and pallets, and containers or trailers suit long-distance freight. The vehicle models page lists the capacity of each.

Do you cover my city?

Location search covers addresses across India, and pickup is arranged from the nearest depot. If a route cannot be driven, the booking form tells you before you confirm.

How do I drive for TruckBhai?

Apply on the drive with us page with your vehicle details and city, and we will call you.
